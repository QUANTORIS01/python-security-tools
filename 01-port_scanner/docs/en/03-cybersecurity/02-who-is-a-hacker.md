# Session 02 — Who Is a Hacker?

# Introduction

When we hear the word:

```text
Hacker
```

what usually comes to mind?

---

Many people imagine:

```text
An unknown person
in a black hoodie
in a dark room
hacking into banks
```

---

But reality is very different from movies and TV series.

---

In the tech world:

```text
Not every hacker is a criminal
and not every security specialist is a hacker
```

---

To understand cybersecurity, we must first understand:

```text
Who is a hacker, really?
```

---

# What Is a Hacker?

In the simplest definition:

---

A hacker is someone who:

```text
Understands systems deeply
```

and can:

```text
Analyze their behavior
```

---

In the past, the word Hacker had no negative connotation at all.

---

In the early decades of computing:

```text
Hacker
```

referred to someone who was:

```text
Creative
Curious
and had high technical knowledge
```

---

# Why Is Hacker Considered Negative Today?

Because the media usually presents:

```text
Cyber Criminals
```

under the label:

```text
Hacker
```

---

As a result, the impression has been created that:

```text
Hacker = Criminal
```

---

While this definition is not accurate.

---

# Types of Hackers

Hackers are usually placed in three main categories.

---

# White Hat Hacker

White hat hacker

---

These people:

```text
Work legally
```

---

Their goal is:

```text
Increasing security
```

---

Example:

---

A company asks a security specialist:

```text
Review our system
```

---

The security specialist:

```text
Finds vulnerabilities
```

---

and reports them.

---

This person is a:

```text
White Hat
```

---

# A Real Example

Many security specialists at large companies:

```text
Google
Microsoft
Amazon
Meta
```

belong to this category.

---

# Black Hat Hacker

Black hat hacker

---

These people:

```text
Without authorization
```

attack systems.

---

Their goals can be:

```text
Stealing information
Extortion
Sabotage
Espionage
```

---

Example:

---

Stealing users' bank card information.

---

Or:

---

Infecting systems with ransomware.

---

# Gray Hat Hacker

Gray hat hacker

---

Falls between the two previous groups.

---

They might:

```text
Without authorization
```

find a security weakness.

---

But:

```text
Without a financial or destructive goal
```

---

Example:

---

A person finds a vulnerability on a website.

---

Then informs the site's administrator.

---

But did not obtain permission beforehand.

---

# Do White Hats Hack?

Yes.

---

In fact:

```text
Hacking
```

itself is not a crime.

---

The key point is:

```text
Authorization
```

---

Example:

---

If the system owner says:

```text
Test my system
```

---

This is:

```text
Penetration Testing
```

---

But if it is done without permission:

---

It may be illegal.

---

# What Is a Red Team?

In large organizations, we see the term:

```text
Red Team
```

---

Task:

```text
Simulating an attacker
```

---

A Red Team tries to:

```text
Enter the system
```

---

Exactly like a real attacker.

---

# What Is a Blue Team?

A Blue Team is responsible for:

```text
Defense
```

---

Tasks:

```text
Monitoring
Detection
Incident Response
Defense
```

---

They try to detect and stop attacks.

---

# What Is a Purple Team?

The combination of:

```text
Red Team
+
Blue Team
```

---

Goal:

```text
Collaborating to increase security
```

---

# What Is a Script Kiddie?

Another famous term:

```text
Script Kiddie
```

---

Someone who:

```text
Runs ready-made tools
```

---

But does not have deep knowledge of how they work.

---

Example:

---

A person runs Nmap.

---

But does not know:

```text
What TCP is
What a Packet is
What a Port is
```

---

# Are We Hackers?

Currently:

---

We are learning.

---

But the project we have built:

```text
Port Scanner
```

is one of the tools that security specialists use.

---

Therefore:

---

We are learning skills that are used in the field of:

```text
Cybersecurity
```

---

# Why Is a Port Scanner Important?

Suppose you are the security officer of a company.

---

The first question:

```text
Which services are active?
```

---

To answer this question:

```text
Port Scanner
```

is used.

---

Therefore, before defense or attack:

```text
Information must be gathered
```

---

# What Is Reconnaissance?

One of the most important stages of security:

```text
Reconnaissance
```

---

Meaning:

```text
Identifying and gathering information
```

---

The port scanner is used exactly at this stage.

---

# Are All Hackers Programmers?

No.

---

```text
Programming
Networking
Operating Systems
Security
```

well.

---

---
